    How to play

    Each chicken starts with one tail feather. Collect them all to win.

    1. Look at the target egg

      Follow the track clockwise. The first empty egg ahead shows the picture to find.

    2. Flip a memory tile

      A match moves your chicken to that egg and lets you flip again. On a miss, stay where you are and pass the turn.

    3. Jump the chickens in your way

      Match the egg beyond them to jump the whole group. Take every feather they hold. A bare-tailed chicken can still win them back.
